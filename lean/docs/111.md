# One-sample matroid prophet inequalities against an almighty adversary

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [One Sample Suffices for Matroid Prophet Inequalities against an Almighty Adversary](../../preprints/One-Sample-Suffices-for-Matroid-Prophet-Inequalities-against-an-Almighty-Adversary-September-23-2026/final.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves a one-sample matroid prophet inequality with expected reward at least $2^{-310}$ times the expected offline optimum. Each element has one independent sample paired with an identically distributed nonnegative online value, and all coordinates are independent. The rule and finite seed law depend only on the labeled matroid; choices are irrevocable and feasible after every prefix. The arrival order may depend measurably on all samples, values, and the seed, and only the offline optimum must be integrable.

A supporting hidden-vector selection result for fixed nonnegative matroid weights gives expected worst-order reward at least $2^{-293}$ times the optimum, again with a finite seed law and prefix feasibility.

### Sharpened constant

The same rule, with re-tuned parameters, proves the stronger ratio $2^{-46}$ (`OneSampleChallengeSharp`, and `HiddenVectorChallengeSharp` for the hidden-vector form); the $2^{-310}$ and $2^{-293}$ statements above are derived from it. The parameters are geometric level ratio $B=2^6$, density charge $\kappa=2^{12}$, guard and test rate $t=2^{-19}$, filter rate $13/16$, density rate $15/32$, and maximum-branch probability $2^{-14}$. The proof keeps the manuscript's architecture and replaces four lossy estimates:

- squared exit mass $6/25$ instead of $2^{-13}$ (`Reverse/ExitDual.lean`, `Reverse/Squared.lean`): duality between $h^2$ and the constraints "expected exit mass is one" and "expected number of non-exit steps with hazard at least $p$ is at most $\log(1/p)$", the latter from Lemma 4.1 started at the first step with hazard at least $p$;
- residual-family count with only the generator occurrences movable in the first marked sequence, and exponent $(3(1+\log\kappa)+2(1+\log(\kappa/2))+3\log 4)/\kappa$ (`Pivots/Entropy.lean`, `Pivots/ResidualFamily.lean`, `Residual/Tail.lean`);
- weighted-count factor $1-1/B$ instead of $1/2$, and the density cost of later groups at rate $15/32$;
- a biased branch coin, with the maximum rule reusing the filter mask.

The secretary corollary and the source contracts are restated for the new rates and remain proved.
